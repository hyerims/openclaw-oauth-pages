Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

About this application

OpenClaw Workspace is a personal AI assistant integration operated for the account owner's own use.

Google account data

With the account owner's authorization, the application may access Google Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Contacts data to perform requested tasks.

How data is used

Google account data is used to respond to the account owner's requests and support personal workflow automation.

Data sharing

This application is not offered as a public service. Authorized data may be processed by connected AI or automation providers when required for requested functionality.

Data storage and security

Authentication credentials and application data may be stored in the owner's local environment. Access is controlled through the owner's device and connected service permissions.

Access revocation

The account owner can revoke Google account access through Google Account security settings.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the application owner through the application's Google OAuth support contact.
